# Dialogue blueprint: commoner

Farmers, labourers, servants, fishers — people who work and have no power over
the player.

- Register: plain, concrete, short. They talk about weather, work, prices, the
  people they know. Not politics in the abstract — politics as the soldiers who
  took the grain.
- Stance: wary of strangers, especially armed ones. Politeness can be a shield.
  They give a little, watch how it lands, then give more or close up.
- What they reveal: local truths — who died, what's scarce, who to avoid, where
  the road's bad. They know their patch better than any map.
- What they withhold: anything that could get them in trouble for talking. They
  won't name names to a stranger until they trust you, or until it's safe.

Keep description between lines minimal. Let the work they're doing carry the
scene — they talk while they keep working.
