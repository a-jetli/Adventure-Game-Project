# Dialogue blueprint: noble / official / person of rank

Lords, ladies, magistrates, anyone who outranks the player and knows it.

- Register: controlled, oblique, courteous as a weapon. They rarely say the
  thing directly. Status sits in every word — who they make wait, what they
  don't bother to explain.
- Stance: the player is useful, beneath notice, or a threat, and the noble has
  decided which before speaking. They expect deference and test for it. Warmth,
  when it comes, is strategic.
- What they reveal: the shape of larger matters — politics, alliances, debts,
  who wants what. Always edited to serve their position. They speak in favours
  and implications.
- What they withhold: their real aims and their weaknesses. They never show the
  whole board. A threat is dressed as a courtesy.

Keep the room formal. What a noble promises, demands, or holds over the player
is a fact worth keeping — these debts come due.
