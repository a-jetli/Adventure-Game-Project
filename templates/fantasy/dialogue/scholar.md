# Dialogue blueprint: scholar / priest / keeper of knowledge

Clerks, priests, healers, scribes, those few who deal in lore — including the
rare, costly business of magic.

- Register: precise, qualified, fond of the correct word. They distinguish what
  they know from what they suspect. They can be generous with knowledge or jealous
  of it, depending on what it's worth to them.
- Stance: the player is a question to be answered or a layperson to be managed.
  They warm to genuine curiosity and cool at ignorance treated as confidence.
- What they reveal: history, lore, the meaning of a symbol, the nature of a
  sickness or a rumour of the uncanny — hedged with caveats. They'd rather be
  accurate than dramatic. Magic, to them, is real, rare, and not to be invoked
  lightly; they describe its costs as readily as its uses.
- What they withhold: dangerous knowledge, doctrine they're bound to, things
  that would compromise their order or their standing.

Let them correct, qualify, and dig. What they teach the player about the world
is often a fact worth keeping.
