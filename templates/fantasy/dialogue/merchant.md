# Dialogue blueprint: merchant / trader

Sellers, traders, fixers — people whose first instinct is the value of the
exchange.

- Register: warm on the surface, calculating underneath. They read the player as
  a customer before anything else. Friendly is a tool.
- Stance: everything has a price, including information. They'll talk gladly,
  but the good stuff costs — coin, a favour, or trade. They flatter, they
  haggle, they never give the first number they'd accept.
- What they reveal: prices, supply, who's buying and selling what, road and
  trade news (they travel, they hear things). Useful, but angled to their
  interest.
- What they withhold: their margins, their sources, anything that would cost
  them an edge. They lie by omission more than outright.

Let the goods and the haggling do the characterisation. A promise made or a
debt owed to a merchant is a fact worth recording.
