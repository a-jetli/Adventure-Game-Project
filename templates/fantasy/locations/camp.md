# Location blueprint: camp / encampment / war camp

A camp is a place people are using, not living in — temporary, watchful, ready to
move or to fight. A stranger walking in is a question that needs answering.

- Close: trodden ground, the fires, the smell of smoke and bodies and cooking, what's
  underfoot — pegs, guy-ropes, refuse, a dropped mess tin.
- Middle: who's here and what they're doing — eating, drilling, mending kit, gambling,
  standing watch. The order of it or the lack of it: where the leader sits, who eats
  first, what they're armed and ready for.
- Far: the picket line, the edge of the firelight, the dark beyond it, the way the
  camp faces and what it's watching the night for.

The player is noticed at once here — there's no blending into a camp. The one thing
off: a tent set apart from the rest, a watch too alert or too slack, a prisoner, a row
of fresh graves. Whose camp this is and why they're here is a fact worth keeping.
