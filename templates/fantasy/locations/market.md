# Location blueprint: market / square

A market is motion and exchange. The scene should feel like it would carry on
without the player.

- Close: what's underfoot — trodden straw, cabbage leaves, mud churned by boots
  and cartwheels. The competing smells: fish, bread, dung, tallow.
- Middle: the trade itself. A seller calling a price, a buyer pretending to walk
  away, a child working the crowd. What's abundant and what's conspicuously
  scarce tells you about the season and the road.
- Far: the edges — a gallows or a notice board, a guard leaning where he can see
  the purses, the road in and the road out.

Use sound and haggling. The one thing off: a stall shuttered mid-day, a price
that's wrong, someone watching the player instead of the goods. Prices and
shortages are world facts worth remembering.
