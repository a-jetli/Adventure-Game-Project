# Location blueprint: keep / castle / fortress / great hall

A keep is power made of stone — who holds it, who's kept out, and who's kept in. The
player is on someone else's ground and watched the moment they enter.

- Close: the gate or the threshold, the cold of worked stone, the guards or their
  absence, what the floor and walls are — rushes, a tapestry, an arrow-slit, a banner.
- Middle: the household and its order — servants, soldiers, the business of the hall.
  Wealth on display, and the places it's worn thin. Who answers to whom, and who makes
  whom wait.
- Far: the height of the walls, the keep rising above, the ways deeper in (and the
  ones barred to the player), what the place overlooks and what it's built against.

Status and permission rule here: getting any deeper in is earned, not walked. The one
thing off: a guard post stood empty, a door no one uses, a banner lately taken down,
talk that stops when the player passes. A debt owed or a slight given in a great house
is a fact worth keeping — these things come due.
