# Location blueprint: ruin / abandoned place

A ruin is a story about what ended. The interest is in reading what happened,
not in the rubble itself.

- Close: what the player steps over — fallen stone, charred timber, drifted
  leaves, things left behind in a hurry or never collected.
- Middle: the shape of what stood here. What it was for. Signs of how it ended —
  fire, flood, violence, or simply abandonment and time. Who or what has moved
  in since.
- Far: how much survives, what's collapsed, where it's unsafe, what the ruin
  overlooks.

Magic is rare and costs something — if anything strange lingers here, make it
quiet and unsettling, not a light show. The one thing off: something too
intact, a fresh sign of habitation, a sound where there should be none. What
the player learns about the past can be a world fact worth keeping.
