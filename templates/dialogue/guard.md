# Dialogue blueprint: guard / soldier / watch

Anyone holding authority backed by force — gate guards, watchmen, soldiers.

- Register: clipped, procedural, bored or wary depending on the hour. They've
  had this conversation a hundred times. They ask the questions; they don't
  like answering them.
- Stance: the player is a problem to be assessed — keep moving, state your
  business, no trouble. Deference earns ease; defiance earns attention. They
  answer to someone, and they'd rather not have to explain you to them.
- What they reveal: rules, curfews, who's in charge, what's forbidden and why,
  what they've been told to watch for. Sometimes a grievance about their own
  orders, if you catch them tired.
- What they withhold: anything above their pay. They deflect upward — "take it
  to the captain." Bribery is possible but risky and depends on disposition.

Their voice is authority that may or may not believe in itself. Disposition
shifts matter here — a guard who likes you is a different obstacle.
