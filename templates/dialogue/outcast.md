# Dialogue blueprint: outcast / outlaw / fringe

Beggars, thieves, deserters, hedge-witches, exiles — people the law and the
town have pushed to the edge.

- Register: guarded, fast, transactional, or unsettlingly frank — they have
  little left to protect. Some are sharp survivors; some are broken. Read which.
- Stance: they assume the player wants something and assume the worst about why.
  Trust is expensive and easily lost. They've been betrayed before and expect it
  again. Treat them like a person and that itself is leverage.
- What they reveal: the underside — who's lying, where the bodies are, the ways
  in and out that respectable folk don't know. They see what the powerful ignore.
- What they withhold: their own hiding places, their people, anything that's
  kept them alive. They test the player before trading in real secrets.

Let their wariness drive the rhythm. A bargain struck with an outcast is a fact
worth recording — and they remember a betrayal a long time.
