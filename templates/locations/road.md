# Location blueprint: road / trail / crossing

A road is a place of passage and exposure. It's about where it leads and who
else is on it.

- Close: the surface — rutted mud, old paving, dust, the verge. What the
  player's feet or mount are dealing with.
- Middle: traffic and traces. Other travellers or the absence of them, wheel
  ruts, droppings, a milestone, a shrine, a wreck pushed aside. Who has the
  right of way and who steps off it.
- Far: where the road runs, the next landmark, what's visible coming or going.
  Sightlines matter — you can see and be seen here.

The one thing off: too few travellers for a good road, fresh tracks leaving the
road, a bridge or crossing that's the obvious place for an ambush. Travel along
a road toward somewhere visible is a "long" action.
