# Location blueprint: dungeon / cave / underground

Underground, the world closes in. Restrict sense and information; let the dark
do the work.

- Close: footing, damp, cold, the air — stale, foul, moving or dead still. What
  the light source reaches and where it fails.
- Middle: the worked or natural structure — tool marks, water-carved stone,
  passages, a threshold. Signs of who dug, built, or denned here, and whether
  they're gone.
- Far: what's beyond the light — a draught from somewhere, a drip, a passage
  going down. What the player can hear but not see.

Sound is everything when sight is gone. The one thing off: a fresh mark, bones
arranged wrong, warmth where it should be cold, a silence that's listening
back. Keep any magic rare and costly. Don't telegraph every danger.
