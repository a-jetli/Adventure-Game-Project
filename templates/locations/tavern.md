# Location blueprint: tavern / inn

A tavern is a social engine, not a backdrop. Foreground people and the heat of
the room over architecture.

- Close: what the player smells first — spilled ale, woodsmoke, wet wool, a
  hearth doing too much or too little. The floor underfoot, the press of bodies.
- Middle: who is here and what they are doing. Someone behind the bar with an
  opinion. A table that goes quiet when the door opens, or doesn't. Coin
  changing hands, a game, an argument running low.
- Far: the stairs, the back room, the door to the yard. Where you'd go to talk
  privately, or leave fast.

Sound carries the scene: a stool scraping, a name said too loud, the lull when
strangers walk in. The one thing off: a seat nobody takes, a stain being
scrubbed, a man drinking like he's waiting for someone who won't come.

Taverns are where rumor lives. Plant talk in the world, don't hand it over.
