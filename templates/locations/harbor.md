# Location blueprint: harbor / docks / waterfront

A harbor is the edge between land and sea — goods, news, and people passing through
rather than staying. It should feel busy with business that isn't the player's.

- Close: the boards underfoot, the slap of water against the pilings, the smell of
  salt and tar and fish and rot, what's stacked and lashed and spilling from crates.
- Middle: the work of the water — boats unloading, nets mended, a crane swinging, the
  press of dockhands and the men who stand watching them. What's coming in and going
  out, and what's moving quietly. Foreign tongues, strange cargo.
- Far: the ships at anchor, the mouth of the harbor, the weather coming in off the
  water, the customs house or the hull rotting at the far mole.

Harbors are where smuggling and rumor live — plant both, and don't hand them over.
Tides and weather are real limits worth honoring. The one thing off: a boat that won't
unload, a hold kept shut, a quiet berth where the law doesn't look. What ships and
cargo pass through can be a world fact worth keeping.
