# Location blueprint: temple / shrine / holy place

A temple is built for something larger than the people in it. The interest is in
reverence — or its absence, neglect, or what's crept in to replace it.

- Close: the threshold and the smell — incense, tallow, cold stone, or rot. What's
  underfoot: worn flagstones, scattered offerings, ash, dried flowers. The hush, or
  the thing breaking it.
- Middle: the focus of the place — an altar, an idol, a relic, a tended flame. The
  signs of worship and whether they're kept up or going to ruin. Who keeps it and who
  comes; what's been given here and what's been taken.
- Far: the height of it, the dark above the candlelight, side chapels and alcoves,
  the way back out, the stair down to the crypt.

If anything uncanny lingers here, keep it rare and quiet — magic costs something, and
a temple earns its weight from restraint, not a light show. The one thing off: an
offering too fresh or too old, a defaced symbol, a door kept locked, a silence that
seems to listen back. What the player learns of the faith or the dead can be a world
fact worth keeping.
