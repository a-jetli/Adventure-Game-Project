# Location blueprint: settlement / town / village

Show a place that was lived in before the player arrived and will be after they
leave. Wear and work over grandeur.

- Close: the ground and the buildings within reach — timber gone grey, thatch
  dark with moss, a doorstep worn into a dish by feet.
- Middle: the life of it. Who's working, who's idle, what the place is for — a
  mill, a forge, nets drying, a field at its edge. Smoke from how many chimneys.
- Far: the road through, the horizon, what the settlement turns its back to and
  what it faces. Defenses or the lack of them.

Show history: what happened here, who passed through, what's been repaired and
what's been left to rot. The one thing off: too few people, a building everyone
avoids, a fresh grave. End on something live.
