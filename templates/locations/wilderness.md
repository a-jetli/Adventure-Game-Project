# Location blueprint: wilderness / forest / wild country

Out here the world is indifferent, not hostile. Make it large and make the
player small in it.

- Close: footing and weather — root, bog, scree, the temperature, what the air
  does to skin. What grows underfoot and whether it's been disturbed.
- Middle: the living country. Birds that go quiet, deer sign, a treeline, the
  sound of water. Evidence of passage: a snare, a cold firepit, a felled tree.
- Far: the lie of the land — ridge, river, the way the ground falls, where
  shelter or trouble would be. Light and how much of it is left.

Lean on sound and smell; sight is limited out here. The one thing off: a too-
quiet stretch, a track that shouldn't be there, weather turning. Don't fill the
wild with monsters by default — emptiness can carry more weight.
